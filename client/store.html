How Your Order Works

  1. You place your order.
  2. We review your request and select the correct kit.
  3. We contact you to confirm if you need pre-configuration or support.
  4. We send you a final invoice with a secure payment link.
  5. Once payment is received, we ship your order based on your selected delivery method.

Important: Please keep an eye on the Notes section of your order. Our team may send updates or ask questions, and you can reply directly there.
